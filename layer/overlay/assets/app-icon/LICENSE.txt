Illostrator app icon (vectorcraft.svg, vectorcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in
this directory: vectorcraft-1024.png, vectorcraft-macos-512.png, vectorcraft.ico, hicolor/).

Original artwork made for Illostrator: a purple rocker cat in sunglasses and a yellow crown of a
mohawk, playing a pink guitar drawn as a vector path (anchors and Bezier handles showing) with a
fountain-pen nib for a pick, on a cream-to-peach field. No third-party material. Licensed like the
rest of the project, under either of

  * Apache License, Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
  * MIT license (https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

at your option.

vectorcraft.icns (macOS only) is not replaced: it is still VectorCraft's icon.

Regenerate the derived files with packaging/icons.sh.
